Privacy Policy for UniLog

Last updated: 29 August 2026

UniLog is a personal training log for unicyclists. This Privacy Policy explains, simply, what the app does with your data: nothing.

No data collection

UniLog does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information of any kind. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-up is required to use the app.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies are used.
  • The app does not require or use an internet connection to function.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — tricks, practice sessions, results, equipment, notes — is stored locally on your own device only. The developer has no access to it, ever.

Exporting your data

If you choose to export your data from within the app, the exported file is created on your device and shared entirely under your own control (for example, via the iOS share sheet). The developer never receives a copy.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all locally stored data along with it, unless you have exported a copy yourself beforehand.
