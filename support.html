UniLog Support

Last updated: 29 August 2026

UniLog is a personal training log for unicyclists — set up which tricks you're working on, run timed practice sessions, and track your progress over time.

Need help, found a bug, or have a suggestion?

Get in touch by email: ola.kare.risa@gmail.com

Please include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem — it helps a lot in tracking things down.

Your data

All your training data stays on your own device — UniLog has no account system and no server. You can export a full backup of your data at any time from Setup, and import it again later or on another device.

See the Privacy Policy for details on what data the app collects (nothing).
